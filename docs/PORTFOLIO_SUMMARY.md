# Portfolio summary

## CV bullet

Designed and prototyped an AI agent catalog and governance console, translating metadata discovery, accountable ownership, risk triage, and lifecycle needs into searchable inventories, contextual agent profiles, and eligibility-aware bulk actions with confirmation and recovery paths.

## Alternative focused on enterprise governance

Developed a product case study for AI agent governance, applying enterprise catalog and stewardship concepts to agent discovery, ownership acknowledgement, risk review, and source-confirmed control outcomes; modeled partial failures and manual follow-up in bulk governance workflows.

## Alternative focused on product management

Framed and prototyped an end-to-end governance operator journey from agent discovery to action verification, documenting personas, prioritization tradeoffs, metadata requirements, and a proposed usability and success-measurement plan.

## Interview walkthrough

1. **Start with the operator's decision.** Explain why an agent name and platform are insufficient without purpose, ownership, and operating context.
2. **Show Agent Search.** Discuss metadata design, exception queues, and the persistent details panel.
3. **Show the bulk preview.** Explain why a cross-platform product must disclose eligibility and manual-control limits.
4. **Show the mixed result.** Separate the requested action from a source-confirmed outcome, then discuss timeout recovery and evidence.
5. **Discuss the next validation.** Explain which user assumptions and workflow comprehension questions should be tested before investing in integrations.
6. **Connect to the market.** Use the dated official sources in INDUSTRY_CONTEXT.md to discuss agent registries, stewardship, lineage, governed context, and operational controls.

## Claim boundaries

The project supports claims about product framing, prototype and interaction design, governance-domain reasoning, and documented hypotheses. It does not support claims of production platform integrations, shipped enforcement services, regulatory certification, validated customer demand, adoption, cost savings, or measured risk reduction.

Any numerical impact in a future CV should come from a documented study or deployment, not from the sample inventory and simulated outcomes in the prototype.
